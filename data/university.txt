The Bachelor of Information Technology (BIT) degree is offered by the University.

Semester Registration

Students must complete semester registration before the deadline. Registration usually begins on August 15.

Semester Schedule

The first semester begins on August 20.

Examinations

Mid examinations are conducted on October 10.

Final examinations are conducted on December 15.

Library

The university library is open from 8:00 AM until 8:00 PM every weekday.

Computer Lab

The computer laboratory is available from 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM.

Hostel

Students can apply for hostel accommodation through the Student Affairs Office.

Student Affairs

The Student Affairs Office handles student ID cards, hostel requests, scholarships and student welfare.

Scholarships

Merit scholarships are awarded based on academic performance.

Graduation

Students who successfully complete the BIT degree are eligible for graduation after meeting all credit requirements.
